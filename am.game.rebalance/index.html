Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

In short. Rebalance is a free, ad-supported word puzzle. No account is required. Your progress lives on your device and, when online, is backed up to Firebase under an anonymous ID. Leaderboards only receive the name you choose and your score. Ads are served by Google AdMob or Yandex Ads depending on your country, and they may use your device's advertising identifier under their own policies. We do not sell data and never ask for contacts, camera, microphone or location.

1. Who processes the data

The developer and data controller is Robert Tadevosyan (independent developer), robtdyn@gmail.com. The app is published on Google Play and the App Store under the id am.game.rebalance.

3. Who receives data

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics) — progress backup, leaderboards, statistics and crash reports. Firebase privacy.

We do not sell personal data or share it with any other third party. Data may be processed on servers outside your country (including the US and the EU) under the policies of the providers above.

4. Permissions and device features

  • Internet — ads, progress backup, leaderboards, language pack downloads. The game is fully playable offline.
  • Tracking permission (iOS App Tracking Transparency) — asked once before ads load; if declined, non-personalised ads are shown.
  • Camera, microphone, contacts and location are not used.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data is removed when you uninstall the app. The progress backup and leaderboard entries are kept until you ask us to delete them: e-mail robtdyn@gmail.com and we will delete everything tied to your anonymous ID within 30 days. Google keeps Analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

6. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Firebase access is restricted by security rules: each user can write only their own progress and their own records.

7. Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed specifically at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Parents may contact us to have data deleted.

8. Your rights

You can uninstall the app at any time (local data), change your leaderboard name in Settings, withdraw consent to personalised ads in your device settings, and request deletion of cloud data by e-mail. Residents of the EEA, the UK, Brazil and California have the right to access, correct, delete and port their data — contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

If data practices change we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be noted in the store release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: robtdyn@gmail.com. Source code: github.com/RobertTadevosyan/Rebalance.

Política de privacidade

Última atualização: 23 de setembro de 2026

Em resumo. Rebalance é um jogo de palavras gratuito mantido por anúncios. Não é preciso conta. Seu progresso fica no seu dispositivo e, com internet, é salvo no Firebase sob um ID anônimo. Os rankings recebem apenas o nome que você escolhe e sua pontuação. Os anúncios são exibidos pelo Google AdMob ou pelo Yandex Ads conforme o país, e eles podem usar o identificador de publicidade do seu dispositivo segundo suas próprias políticas. Não vendemos dados e nunca pedimos contatos, câmera, microfone ou localização.

1. Quem trata os dados

O desenvolvedor e controlador dos dados é Robert Tadevosyan (desenvolvedor independente), robtdyn@gmail.com. O app é publicado no Google Play e na App Store com o id am.game.rebalance.

  • Internet – anúncios, backup do progresso, rankings, download de pacotes de idioma. O jogo funciona totalmente offline.

Todo o tráfego usa HTTPS. O acesso ao Firebase é restrito por regras de segurança: cada usuário só pode gravar o próprio progresso e os próprios recordes.
